# Fjaum: player manual

Fjaum is a small monster-collecting role-playing game for the Game Boy, Game
Boy Color, Master System, Game Gear and PC Engine. The monsters are called
Fjaum. This is **Chapter 1** of a game planned in four chapters: the town of
Tessel and the country around it.

Words in CAPITALS are written the way the game shows them.

## The story so far

You live in Tessel with your mom. One morning she greets you with "YOU'RE UP!
THE ELDER ASKED FOR YOU." The Elder lives in the house with the green roof,
across the road. When you visit him he says the keystone lit up in your hand,
that you are the one, and that you should take a Fjaum along.

Wren, your rival, has a Fjaum too and means to win the cup. On the way you
will meet other trainers, and in the woods a shrine whose Keeper says your
keystone woke it.

## Controls

| Action | Game Boy / Color | Game Gear | Master System | PC Engine |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Walk, move a menu cursor | D-pad | D-pad | D-pad | D-pad |
| Talk, confirm, close a text box | A | button 2 | button 2 | I |
| Back, cancel a menu | B | button 1 | button 1 | II |
| Jump (on the Ridgeway) | A | button 2 | button 2 | I |
| Pause menu | START | START | PAUSE (on the console) | RUN |

To talk to someone, face them and press A. The Ridgeway is seen from the
side: there A jumps, unless you face someone. The pause menu opens in the
town, the routes and the houses, never during a conversation or a battle. On
the Master System it is the PAUSE button on the console itself, not the pad.

## Starting a game

The title screen offers **NEW GAME**, and also **CONTINUE** when the cartridge
holds a saved journey. NEW GAME starts you at home. CONTINUE puts you back
where you saved, with everything you had.

You start with 3 TONICS, 5 SHARDS and 100 gold.

## Fjaum and their elements

Every Fjaum has an **element**. The elements of Chapter 1 are LEAF, TIDE, CRAG
and FLAME; a fifth, PRISM, belongs to Fjaum you do not meet yet. Each of the
four beats one and is weak against another:

| Attacking move | Super effective against | Not very effective against |
| --- | --- | --- |
| LEAF | TIDE | FLAME |
| TIDE | CRAG | LEAF |
| CRAG | FLAME | TIDE |
| FLAME | LEAF | CRAG |

A super effective move does double damage ("IT'S SUPER EFFECTIVE!"), a not
very effective one half ("IT'S NOT VERY EFFECTIVE..."). Everything else,
including RAM, does normal damage.

The Fjaum you can meet in Chapter 1:

| Fjaum | Element | Where | Moves it learns |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| MOSSLING | LEAF | a first Fjaum; Mossway, Mosswood | RAM, VINE LASH, BRACE, BRAMBLE |
| MOSSBACK | LEAF | the Moss Keeper | RAM, VINE LASH, BRACE, BRAMBLE |
| BURRBIT | LEAF | Mossway, Mosswood | RAM, VINE LASH, GLOWER, BRAMBLE |
| BUDWICK | LEAF | Mosswood | RAM, VINE LASH, BRACE, BRAMBLE |
| EMBERKIT | FLAME | a first Fjaum | RAM, CINDER, GLOWER, SCORCH |
| BUBBUN | TIDE | a first Fjaum | RAM, DOUSE, GLOWER, UNDERTOW |
| PADLING | TIDE | Mossway | RAM, DOUSE, GLOWER, UNDERTOW |
| PEBBLIT | CRAG | Mossway, the Ridgeway | RAM, ROCK TOSS, BRACE, STONEFALL |
| CLAYFERN | CRAG | the Moss Keeper, the pilgrim | RAM, ROCK TOSS, BRACE, STONEFALL |
| SCREELET | CRAG | the Ridgeway | RAM, ROCK TOSS, BRACE, STONEFALL |
| QUARRAPEDE | CRAG | the alpinist | RAM, ROCK TOSS, BRACE, STONEFALL |

Every Fjaum knows RAM and its element move from level 1, learns its third
move at level 4 and its fourth at level 10.

**Stats.** HP is its health. ATK and DEF count for RAM; the element moves use
FOC (focus) on both sides instead. SPD decides who moves first.

## Your first Fjaum

Talk to the Elder and choose **MOSSLING**, **EMBERKIT** or **BUBBUN**. It
starts at level 5. Wren then picks the one strong against yours.

Until you have a Fjaum, no wild Fjaum appear, Wren turns you back on the path
through Mossway ("THE GRASS IS RISKY!"), and the other trainers tell you to
come back with one.

## Battles

Wild Fjaum hide in Mossway's tall grass, Mosswood's ferns and the Ridgeway's
heath grass. They only appear while you are walking there. Right after a
battle you get a few safe steps.

Your **lead** Fjaum fights; the others wait. If the lead faints, the battle is
lost. Each turn you choose a command:

- **FIGHT** shows the lead's moves. An empty slot shows `-`. B goes back.
- **CATCH** throws a shard (see below). Not against a trainer: "YOU CAN'T
  CATCH A TRAINER'S FJAUM!"
- **ITEM** uses a TONIC on your lead: 20 HP back.
- **RUN** escapes a wild battle three times in four ("GOT AWAY SAFELY." or
  "COULDN'T GET AWAY!"). Never from a trainer: "NO RUNNING FROM A TRAINER!"

With FIGHT, the faster Fjaum moves first (a tie is chance). After CATCH, ITEM
or a failed RUN, the foe moves.

A strong blow can be "A CRITICAL HIT!" (double damage, the screen flashes). A
defeated foe "SHATTERED INTO SPARKLES!"

### Moves

| Move | Element | Uses | What it does |
| --- | --- | :-: | --- |
| RAM | none | 35 | a plain hit with ATK |
| VINE LASH | LEAF | 25 | a hit with FOC; may poison (1 in 5) |
| DOUSE | TIDE | 25 | a hit with FOC; may put to sleep (1 in 7) |
| ROCK TOSS | CRAG | 25 | a hit with FOC; may crack (1 in 5) |
| CINDER | FLAME | 25 | a hit with FOC; may burn (1 in 5) |
| GLOWER | - | 30 | the foe's power falls |
| BRACE | - | 30 | your guard rises |
| BRAMBLE | LEAF | 15 | a strong hit with ATK; may poison (1 in 5) |
| UNDERTOW | TIDE | 15 | a strong hit with ATK; may put to sleep (1 in 7) |
| STONEFALL | CRAG | 15 | a strong hit with ATK; may crack (1 in 5) |
| SCORCH | FLAME | 15 | a strong hit with ATK; may burn (1 in 5) |

The four strong moves hit 9 times in 10 ("BUT IT MISSED!"); every other move
always hits.

Each move can be used a limited number of times ("NO USES LEFT FOR THAT
MOVE!"). When every move is used up, your Fjaum rams anyway ("NO USES LEFT AT
ALL! IT RAMS."). Healing refills every move.

### Status effects

| Effect | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| BURNED | its RAM does half damage |
| POISONED | it loses an eighth of its HP at the end of every turn |
| ASLEEP | it skips its turns for 2 or 3 turns ("FAST ASLEEP") |
| CRACKED | its DEF is halved, so RAM hurts it more |
| POWER FELL / ROSE | its attacks are weaker / stronger, up to two steps each way |
| GUARD FELL / ROSE | it takes more / less damage, up to two steps each way |

At the limit the game says "NOTHING HAPPENED." Power and guard steps and sleep
end with the battle. A burn, poison or crack on your Fjaum stays until it is
healed.

### Catching

Choose CATCH, then a shard: **SHARD**, **GLINT SHARD** or **PRISM SHARD**.
"YOU RAISE THE KEYSTONE...", the screen shakes up to three times, and then either
"GOTCHA! IT JOINED YOUR PARTY!" or "IT BROKE FREE!" (then the foe moves).
With a full party: "GOTCHA! BUT YOUR PARTY'S FULL, SO YOU LET IT GO."

The chance is better the less HP the foe has left, with a better shard (a
GLINT SHARD counts one and a half times a SHARD, a PRISM SHARD twice), when
the foe is burned, poisoned or cracked (more when it is asleep) and with a
LURE GEM on your lead. Some Fjaum are easier to catch than others.

### Winning and losing

A win gives your lead XP (the foe's level x 6, plus 4; a refracted foe
gives more: x 9 for a second form, x 12 for a third) and, from a wild
Fjaum, gold (the foe's level x 3: "YOU FOUND ... GOLD."). Sometimes a wild
Fjaum drops a gem (one win in eight). A trainer pays gold when you have beaten
all of their Fjaum.

If your lead faints: "YOUR FJAUM FAINTED..." and "YOU HURRY HOME TO REST."
You wake at home with the whole party healed and keep your gold. A trainer you
lost to can be challenged again.

## Your party

You carry up to **three** Fjaum. A Fjaum caught while the party is full does
not join it: you let it go ("GOTCHA! BUT YOUR PARTY'S FULL, SO YOU LET IT
GO."). It still counts as caught in the DEX. There is no storage.

Mom and the Elder heal the whole party: HP, status effects and move uses.

### The party cards and the LEAD

Pause menu, **PARTY**: one card per Fjaum, starting with the lead. A card has
two pages:

1. name and level, HP, ATK, XP and the XP the next level needs, DEF;
2. its facet, SPD, FOC and its gem. The lead's second page starts with
   **LEAD**.

Then choose **NEXT** (the next card), **SOCKET** (give it a gem) or **LEAD**
("... LEADS THE PARTY."). The lead is the Fjaum that fights, earns XP and
uses its gem, so pick it before you walk into the grass or talk to a trainer.

### Levels and learning moves

A Fjaum needs its level x 12 XP for the next level ("LEVEL UP! NOW LV ...").
Its stats grow with every level, up to the chapter's level limit: level 15 in
Chapter 1. At the limit a win gives no XP ("... CAN'T GROW PAST L15 YET.");
the next chapter raises it. When it reaches a level where it learns a move,
it learns it into a free slot: "IT LEARNED ...!"

A Fjaum has four move slots. If all four are full when it meets a new move,
the game asks "... WANTS TO LEARN .... FORGET WHICH?" Pick the move to forget,
or press B to keep all four ("IT DID NOT LEARN ...."). In Chapter 1 a Fjaum
learns four moves at most, so this question does not come up yet.

### Refraction

When your lead's level reaches its refraction level, it refracts after the
battle: "WHAT? YOUR ... IS REFRACTING!", the screen flashes, "IT BECAME ...!"
It keeps its moves and the damage it has taken, and the new form counts as
seen and caught in the DEX.

| Fjaum | Level 12 | Level 24 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MOSSLING | MOSSBACK | THORNWYRM |
| EMBERKIT | KILNBAT | LAVAMOLE |
| BUBBUN | RIPTUSK | CORALISK |
| PEBBLIT | CRAGOAT | GEODRAKE |
| BURRBIT | THISTLEHARE | - |
| BUDWICK | PETALISK | - |
| SCREELET | QUARRAPEDE | - |

CLAYFERN and PADLING do not refract.

### Facets

Every Fjaum gets a facet when you receive it or catch it (foes have one too).
A facet raises one stat a little and lowers another: FIERCE (ATK up, FOC
down), DARING (ATK, SPD), STOUT (DEF, ATK), STEADY (DEF, SPD), FLEET (SPD,
DEF), EAGER (SPD, FOC), KEEN (FOC, ATK), WISE (FOC, DEF). EVEN changes
nothing.

### Gems

A gem is socketed into one Fjaum (PARTY, then SOCKET on its card; REMOVE takes
it out, and a gem you replace goes back to your bag). Only the lead's gem works
in battle.

| Gem | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| SPARK GEM | critical hits three times as often |
| MOSS GEM | LEAF moves do a quarter more damage |
| TIDE GEM | TIDE moves do a quarter more damage |
| STONE GEM | CRAG moves do a quarter more damage |
| EMBER GEM | FLAME moves do a quarter more damage |
| MEND GEM | a little HP back at the end of every turn ("THE MEND GEM RESTORED SOME HP.") |
| LURE GEM | catching is easier |
| WIND GEM | RUN always works |

Gems come from wild Fjaum ("IT DROPPED A ...!"), from the Moss Keeper and from
the Elder.

## The pause menu

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| PARTY | the party cards (above) |
| ITEMS | your TONICS and shards; TONIC gives your lead 20 HP back, SHARD explains catching, GEMS lists your gems |
| DEX | four Fjaum a page; MORE turns the page, BACK leaves. A Fjaum you have not seen shows `?????`; one you have seen shows its number, element and SEEN or CAUGHT |
| TRAINER | your gold, how many Fjaum you have SEEN and OWNED, your badges |
| SAVE | saves your journey |
| CLOSE | closes the menu (B does too) |

## Trainers and badges

A trainer shows a "!" and challenges you. Their Fjaum come one after another,
and your lead faces them all. Once beaten, a trainer only talks.

| Trainer | Where | Prize |
| --- | --- | --- |
| WREN | Mossway | 50 gold |
| FARMHAND | Mossway | 60 gold |
| FORAGER | Mosswood | 80 gold |
| PILGRIM | Mosswood | 100 gold |
| MOSS KEEPER | the shrine's hall | 300 gold, the MOSS badge, a MOSS GEM |
| ALPINIST | the Ridgeway | 150 gold |

The **MOSS badge** is the badge of Chapter 1.

The Elder rewards you for the Fjaum you have caught (your first Fjaum and
every refracted form count). Talk to him when you have caught 4, 8, 12 and 16
kinds: 3 GLINT SHARDS, A LURE GEM, 2 PRISM SHARDS and 1000 GOLD.

## Tessel Shop

The clerk sells TONIC (30 G), SHARD (50 G), GLINT (a GLINT SHARD, 120 G) and
PRISM (a PRISM SHARD, 250 G). LEAVE finishes. Without enough gold: "SORRY,
THAT'S NOT ENOUGH GOLD."

## The thorns on the Ridgeway

Part of the way along the Ridgeway, "THICK THORNS BAR THE WAY." Jumping does
not get you past them. To clear them you need:

- the **MOSS badge** from the Moss Keeper, and
- a **LEAF** Fjaum anywhere in your party (it does not have to lead).

Then the game says that Fjaum "CAN HEW THE THORNS." Choose **HEW** and the
thorns are gone for good; LEAVE keeps them for later. Past them lie the rest
of the Ridgeway and the way up to the Ridgeway top.

## Saving and continuing

Choose **SAVE** in the pause menu. You need a Fjaum first. The game keeps ONE
journey: if one is saved already it asks "SAVE OVER YOUR LAST JOURNEY?" (YES
or NO). "YOUR JOURNEY WAS SAVED." confirms it. A save keeps the room, where
you stand, your party, items, gold, DEX and everything you have done.

To go on, choose **CONTINUE** on the title screen.

Chapter 1 is the first part of one journey. The next chapter continues from
your save: your Fjaum, their levels and moves, your items, gold, badges and
DEX come with you.

The PC Engine version cannot save: it answers "THIS SYSTEM CANNOT SAVE."
